About Us

Experienced Guidance. A More Personal Approach.

Olive Branch Accounting was founded on the belief that exceptional financial expertise shouldn't come at the expense of a personal relationship.

After building her career as a certified public accountant at a Big Four accounting firm, Olivia Barrus saw an opportunity to do things differently—to bring the knowledge, rigor, and experience of a large firm into an environment where clients could have a direct relationship with the person actually advising them.

At Olive Branch, that relationship matters.

You aren't passed between departments or left wondering who to call when a question comes up. You work directly with an experienced accounting professional who knows your finances, understands your goals, and can explain the why behind the numbers.

Because good accounting is about more than keeping records or filing returns. It's about having accurate, organized information—and the right guidance—to make better decisions, stay prepared, and move confidently toward what's next.

Big-Firm Experience. One-on-One Attention.

Olive Branch combines the standards and expertise developed through years in Big Four accounting with the accessibility of an independent practice.

That means sophisticated financial knowledge without unnecessary complexity—and an advisor who is genuinely invested in understanding your situation.

Expertise when it matters. Clarity when you need it. A relationship you can rely on.
